# Content: voice, headlines, numbers

## Voice

**A presenter, loud and clear.** We state the problem plainly and the solution confidently. Empathy without drama: people are *lost* between steps, not in *panic*; nobody is blamed.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Her report is here. Her next step isn't." | "Her report arrived. No one explained it." (blames someone) |
| "Plenty of data. Few clear next steps." | "Data chaos is killing patients." |
| "Agents that guide what's next." | "Revolutionary AI-powered synergy." |

The recurring thread: **every result should come with a clear next step.**

## Headlines

- One idea, under ~8 words. Sentence case. A full stop when it's a statement.
- Colour exactly one phrase: `<em>` (blue, or the partner ink in partner sections).
- Kicker above when context helps ("Scene 09", "Where we're going").
- In films, one size for every headline (`--t-scene`, 60px), bottom-centred in the safe area.
- No tone tags, codes or clip IDs in anything a voice-over tool will read aloud.

## Numbers

- Indian formats: ₹, lakh/crore grouping (`1,50,000`, `₹300 Cr`), "Cr" with a space. `toLocaleString('en-IN')`.
- Units small and after the number (`15M+`, `42 min`), tabular numerals.
- Numbers in examples and templates are **illustrative** and get replaced per use case. Use plausible values, or `[N]`, `[X%]`, `[₹]`, `[date]` (styled with `.hd-ph`) when you want the gap to be obvious.
- Anything going public uses confirmed figures; keep a note of each number's source for that piece.

## Names

- Product: **HeyDoc AI**. Tagline: **The Agentic Care OS**. URL: **heydoc.co.in**. (Not heydoc.ai.)
- Agents are named by job: Care, Advisor, Booking, Report, Follow-up, Vision, Queue, Ops, Sales, Marketing, Management, Lab Ops, Logistics.
- Never put a real person's name, a real patient or a real hospital in a mockup unless approved. Use "Admin", "City Diagnostics", "Sunita Ji".
